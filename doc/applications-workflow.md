# Workflow

The eviDense UV Photometer is designed to be integrated into automated liquid handling workflows. The liquid handler handles sample preparation and liquid transfer, while the eviDense UV Photometer performs the optical measurement.

## Typical Measurement Sequence

1. Prepare a microtiter plate containing at least one blank and the samples to be measured.
2. Aspirate at least 10 µL of blank or sample liquid with a pipette tip.
3. Pick up an eviEtte Micro cuvette from the cuvette rack by inserting the pipette tip into the cuvette far enough to create a secure fit.
4. Confirm that the cuvette guide is empty (Command “IsCuvetteHolderEmpty”).
5. Start the baseline measurement.
6. Move the cuvette to the measurement position of the eviDense UV Photometer and insert it into the cuvette guide.
7. Perform the air measurement
8. Dispense the liquid into the cuvette and perform the sample measurement.
9. If the sample is to be retained, either aspirate it back into the pipette tip or push it through the bottom of the cuvette into a designated destination well.
10.	Discard the cuvette and, if applicable, the pipette tip.

The blank is the sample buffer without the analyte. Keep the programmed sequence and the sample identifiers aligned with the physical plate order to ensure that the recorded results remain traceable.
